You sign in with your phone number. We send a code by text message — no password to remember.

Signing in

  1. Tap “Open the app”

    It opens in your browser. There is nothing to install first.

  2. Type the phone number the centre has for you

    The same one we call you on. Any format is fine — 012-345 6789 or +60 12-345 6789 both work.

    “We do not have that number on file” Then we are holding a different number for your family. Tell anyone at the centre and they will fix it in a moment.

  3. Enter the 6-digit code

    It arrives by text message, usually within a few seconds. It comes from a short number rather than from Star Swim — Malaysian operators replace the sender — but the message names us.

  4. That is it

    Your children, their classes and your account are already there. You will not have to do this again on this phone.

Keep it one tap away

Adding it to your home screen gives it its own icon, opens it full screen with no address bar, and means you never have to find the link again.

  1. Open it in Safari

    This only works in Safari on an iPhone. If you are in Chrome, copy the address and open it in Safari first.

  2. Tap the Share button

    The square with an arrow coming out of the top. It sits at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, at the top on an iPad.

  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”

    It is partway down the grey list, under the row of apps. Then tap Add at the top right.

  1. Open it in Chrome

    If a bar appears at the bottom offering to install it, tap that and you are done — the next two steps are for when it does not.

  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right

    Three dots stacked vertically, beside the address bar.

  3. Tap “Install app”, or “Add to Home screen”

    Either wording means the same thing. Confirm, and the icon appears with your other apps.

Signed in on one phone, want it on another? Do the same thing there. Your account works on as many phones as you like, and you can share it with the other parent — the code goes to whichever number we hold.

If something is not right

Speak to anyone at the centre, or call us. Most sign-in trouble is one wrong digit in the number we hold for you, and it takes seconds to put right.
