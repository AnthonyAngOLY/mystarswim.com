Getting parents onto the app
For reception, sales, customer service and the scheduler

It takes about forty seconds

There is nothing to create, nothing to send and no account to set up. Every family already has one. All a parent needs is the link and their own phone number.

The one sentence to remember “Go to mystarswim.com/get on your phone, type your number, and we send you a code by text message.”

What actually happens

So you can answer confidently rather than guessing:

  1. They open the link

    It shows them how to install it and a button into the app.

  2. They type their phone number

    We check it against the number on their family account before anything is sent. A number we do not hold gets nothing — no code, no account created, no charge to us.

  3. A 6-digit code arrives by SMS

    From a short number, not from a Star Swim number — Malaysian operators replace the sender on business messages. The text itself names us.

  4. They are in, for good

    The app links itself to their family. They will not sign in again on that phone.

Say this

At the counter, with the parent in front of you — hand them the card and say roughly this. Do not read it out; it is a shape, not a script.

“You can see your classes, your child’s progress notes from the coach, your credits and your receipts on your phone now. Scan this and put in your phone number — the one we call you on — and you’ll get a code by text. Takes under a minute. Shall we do it while you’re here?”

Do it while they are standing there A parent who sets it up at the counter is a parent who is on it. One who takes a card home mostly is not. If they are waiting for a class to finish anyway, that is the moment.

Send this

For WhatsApp, or anywhere else. Tap to copy.

Hi {name}, Star Swim now has an app for parents 🏊 You can see your class times, your coach’s progress notes, your remaining credits and your receipts — all in one place. Set it up here: https://www.mystarswim.com/get Just put in this phone number and we’ll send you a code by SMS. No password needed. Any trouble, just reply to this message and we’ll sort it out.

Shorter, for a reminder to someone who has not done it yet:

Hi {name}, a reminder that you can now see {child}’s classes, progress and receipts on your phone: https://www.mystarswim.com/get Takes under a minute — just your phone number and a code by SMS.
Replace {name} and {child} before sending A message that arrives saying “Hi {name}” tells the parent we sent it without looking at it.

When it does not work

Nearly all of it is one of these four, and you can fix the first one yourself.

“We do not have that number on file”

We are holding a different number for that family — or the same number written in a way the system cannot match.

Fix it in the Scheduler: Students → find the family → Edit contact → correct the Phone → Save. Ask them to try again straight away; it works immediately.

Type it however it is written — 012-345 6789 is fine. What matters is that the digits are right.

“That number is on two accounts”

Two families are recorded against one phone number, so the app cannot tell whose children to show — and it refuses rather than guess.

Usually two siblings entered as separate families, or a parent who gave the same number for a cousin. This one needs the office to merge them. Take the parent’s name and tell them it will be sorted today; do not leave them trying.

“That number is already linked to a different sign-in”

Someone has already claimed that family on another phone. Almost always the other parent, who set it up and forgot to mention it.

Ask whether a spouse or grandparent may have done it. If genuinely nobody has, it may be a number the telco reissued, and the office needs to unlink it.

The code never arrives

Check in this order:

1. Is the number they typed the one we hold? One wrong digit sends the code to a stranger.
2. Give it a minute. It is usually seconds, but not always.
3. Have them look for a message from a short number rather than from “Star Swim”. Operators replace the sender, so it will not appear under our name and is easy to scroll past.

Only six codes can be sent to one number per hour. A parent who has been tapping repeatedly should wait rather than keep trying.

What not to promise

Do not say it is on the App Store or Play Store It is not, and a parent who goes looking will not find it and will assume we got it wrong. It opens in their browser and they add it to their home screen — that is the whole installation.
Do not set it up on your own phone “for them” The first phone to sign in claims that family permanently. If you do it on a staff phone, the parent cannot — and unpicking it is an office job.

What a parent can see

So nobody promises something that is not there. Six sections:

🏠
Home
Their children, the next class, anything urgent from us.
📅
Classes
Upcoming classes with day, time and pool.
📈
Progress
Every coach’s remark, newest first, with who wrote it and when.
⭐
Credits
Classes remaining per swimmer.
🧾
Billing
Invoices and receipts, paid or outstanding.
✉️
Inbox
Class reminders, absence notices, payment confirmations.

They cannot book, cancel or change anything from the app. It shows; it does not do. Anything a parent wants changed still comes through you.

They can tell us an absence is wrong If a coach marks a swimmer absent by mistake, the parent can tap “This isn’t right” on that message. It appears in the Scheduler under Parents → Sent → Disputed. Worth checking daily — it is the one thing in the app that asks something of us.
