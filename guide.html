Getting in

There is no password and nothing to download from an app store. You sign in with your phone number, once.

“We do not have that number on file” We are holding a different number for your family — often an old one, or a digit out. Tell anyone at the centre and it is fixed in a moment. Nothing is wrong with your phone.
The message comes from a number you will not recognise Malaysian operators replace the sender on messages like this with a short number of their own, so it will not arrive under our name. The message itself says Star Swim. That is normal, and it is us.

Putting it on your home screen

Optional, and worth the twenty seconds. It gets its own icon, opens full screen without the address bar, and you never have to find the link again.

  • On an iPhone

    Open it in Safari → tap Share (the square with an arrow out of the top) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add.

  • On Android

    Open it in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu, top right → Install app or Add to Home screen. Chrome often offers this by itself in a bar at the bottom.

Finding your way around

Six buttons along the bottom. That is the whole app — everything is one tap from here.

  • 1Home Your children and what is next.
  • 2Classes Day, time and pool, ahead.
  • 3Progress What the coaches have written.
  • 4Credits Classes left, per swimmer.
  • 5Billing Invoices and receipts.
  • 6Inbox Messages from the centre.
The red dot On the Inbox, it means something is unread. It disappears once you have opened it.

What each section holds

🏠 Home

Each of your swimmers, and the next class coming up. Anything important from the centre sits at the top until you have read it.

📅 Classes

The classes ahead, with the day, the time and which pool. If a class is cancelled it says so here.

📈 Progress

Every remark a coach has written about your swimmer, newest first and grouped by month, with the coach’s name and the date. Replacement classes with a different coach appear under that coach’s name, so you can follow the whole picture rather than one coach’s view of it.

With more than one child, a filter at the top narrows it to one swimmer.

⭐ Credits

How many classes each swimmer has left. One is used when they attend — and also when they are marked absent without notice, which is why the absence messages matter.

🧾 Billing

Your invoices and your receipts. A receipt is your proof of payment; it stays here, so there is nothing to keep hold of.

✉️ Inbox

Everything we send you, kept. Nothing is ever only a message that scrolls away.

If a missed class is wrong

Coaches mark attendance poolside, and now and then a swimmer who had arranged a replacement is marked absent by mistake.

On that message in your Inbox there is a “This isn’t right” button. One tap tells the centre to look at it — you do not have to telephone or explain. If it was our error, we correct it, your credit comes back, and you get a message saying so.

Please use it It is the fastest way to put an attendance record straight, and it is far better than a family quietly believing we have their attendance wrong.

Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. It is not on the App Store or Play Store and you will not find it there. It opens in your browser, and adding it to your home screen is the whole installation.

Can both parents use it?

Yes, on as many phones as you like. The code goes to the number we hold for your family — so whoever has that phone signs in, and the app then works on that device permanently.

I changed my phone number.

Tell the centre the new one. Until we have it, the code has nowhere to go.

I got a new phone.

Open mystarswim.com/get on it and sign in the same way. Your old phone can stay signed in; it makes no difference.

Can I book or cancel a class in the app?

Not yet. The app shows you everything but changes nothing — bookings, cancellations and replacements still go through the centre.

Is my family’s information safe?

You only ever see your own family. Signing in needs a code sent to the number we already hold, so somebody who knows your phone number still cannot get in without your phone.
