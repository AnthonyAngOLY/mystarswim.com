The coach app
Checking in · the register · progress notes

Everything you do on the pool deck

Check in when you arrive, mark who is in the water, and write a note home when a swimmer does something worth telling their parent about. That is the whole app.

Where it lives attendance.mystarswim.com — add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app. Sign in once with your staff ID and password; it remembers you after that.

Arriving: Today

The first screen lists your shifts for today, in order. Each one is a card with its time, the pool, and one button.

The colour tells you where you are from across the deck, without reading anything. Done and greyed out means that shift is finished.

It records where you were Checking in takes your location and shows it on the card. That is not about trust — it is what settles a question months later when nobody remembers. If it says you were far from the pool, your phone's GPS was confused; check in anyway and mention it.
Leaving early asks you to confirm Checking out on time goes straight through. Checking out well before the session ends asks first, because that one is usually a mis-tap.

The register: Classes

The 🏊 Classes tab appears when you have a class to mark. Each swimmer has two buttons:

Here Away

Tap one. That is it — it saves immediately, there is nothing to submit.

Tapped the wrong one? Tap it again. Tapping the state a swimmer is already in undoes it and puts them back to unmarked. The class credit comes back with it. That is the whole correction, and you can do it any time that day.

What a mark costs the family

Here and Away both use one class credit. Unmarked uses none. That is why an absence matters and why undoing one returns the credit — the family is not charged for a class you marked by mistake.

If a swimmer has no credits left, the app asks before taking one rather than refusing. You are the person standing next to them; mark them and say to have a word at the desk.

After midnight it is not yours to change

You can only mark on the day. After that the app says "That day has closed. Ask the office to change it." — which is deliberate, so a mistake reaches somebody rather than being quietly rewritten a week later. The office can correct any day.

The pencil: progress notes

The ✎ beside a swimmer's name opens a note for that swimmer.

Their parent reads it. Straight away. It is not an internal remark. It appears in the family's app the moment you save it, under your name, with the date. Write it as if you were saying it to them at the side of the pool — because that is what it is.

Good notes are specific and short:

Yes. “Swam a full width unaided today for the first time. Still turning her head late on the breath — we will work on that next week.”

No. “Good session.” — true of every session, and tells the parent nothing they did not assume.

You can see what other coaches have written for the same swimmer, and edit your own. That matters when you are covering: read the last two notes before the lesson and you can pick up where their usual coach left off rather than starting again.

Correcting a note Tap edit on one of your own and change it in place. A note cannot be emptied — if it should not have been written, withdraw it instead.

When the app asks you something

If a shift was late, ended early, or has no check-in or check-out recorded, a box appears on the card asking why. One tap on a reason is usually enough.

⏰
You were marked late
Traffic, cover running over, anything — it is recorded, not judged.
🚪
You left before the session ended
Often the class genuinely finished early. Say so.
❓
No check-in or check-out recorded
Usually a forgotten tap. Answer it and it is closed.

Answer them the same day if you can. An unanswered one keeps appearing, and the office is left guessing on your behalf.

Things that catch people out

I forgot to check in and the session has started

Check in now. The app records that it was late and asks why — answer it and that is the end of it. Not checking in at all is the thing that makes work for somebody else.

I am covering a class that is not mine

You can mark any class running in water you are rostered to at that hour. If the app says you are not rostered to it, the roster has not been updated — the office needs to do that, and until they have, nobody but the named coach can mark it.

A swimmer is here but not on my list

Teach them. The list comes from the schedule, so a missing name is a scheduling matter, not a poolside one. Tell the office after the session.

The app looks out of date

Close it completely and open it again — not just switch away. It updates itself on opening.

I marked someone absent and they had arranged a replacement

Same day: tap Away again to undo it and the credit returns. After midnight: tell the office. They can correct it, the credit comes back, and the family is told it was corrected.
