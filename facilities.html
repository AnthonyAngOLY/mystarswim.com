Our centre

Somewhere you are happy to wait

A swimming lesson is forty minutes for your child and forty minutes for you. We built the centre around both — warm water, clean changing rooms, and somewhere comfortable to sit where you can still see the pool.

Photo: the warm-water pool Wide shot, toddlers in the water, steam or sunlight if you can get it. 4:3.

Water warm enough for a toddler

Cold water is the single most common reason a small child decides they do not like swimming. Ours is heated and held there, so a first lesson is about confidence rather than about shivering.

It matters most for the under-fives, and it is the thing parents notice first.

Changing rooms you do not have to apologise for

Hot showers, dry floors, lockers, hooks and bench space — and enough of it that a parent changing two children is not queuing or balancing a bag on a wet bench.

Cleaned through the day, not just at closing.

A seat with a view of your swimmer

Proper seating, shade, and a clear line of sight to the water — so you can watch the lesson, or get on with something while still glancing up.

Wi-Fi, somewhere to put a coffee, and room for a pram.

Someone watching, always

Small classes mean a coach is close enough to reach any swimmer. Rescue equipment is at the poolside, not in a cupboard, and our coaches hold current certification.

You should be able to relax in the waiting area. That only works if the poolside is genuinely covered.

Come and look before you decide

You are welcome to visit, walk through the changing rooms and watch a lesson from the waiting area. Most parents decide in about ten minutes.
